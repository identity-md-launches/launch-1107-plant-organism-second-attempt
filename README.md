# PLANT ORGANISM

Deploy only this immutable application on Robinhood Chain (4663) through `launch.json`: no token, hook, pool, admin, upgrade or pause. Nonpayable constructor: `(address imd_, address intake_, bytes32 action_, address signer_, uint32 originCell_, address deployer_)`; the manifest preserves the supplied IMD, Intake, action and signer, sets `ORIGIN_CELL = 10223578`, and uses `$owner` for the deployer.

**Sintra: Its gardens bring native and exotic trees together on forested hills.** This sentence is the public constant `ORIGIN`. [UNESCO's Sintra coordinates](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/723/maps/) are N38°46′59.988″, W9°25′0.012″ (38.78333, −9.41667). Floor each coordinate divided by 0.25: latitude index 155, longitude −38. Pack `uint32(uint16(int16(155))) << 16 | uint16(int16(-38))` = `0x009bffda` = `10223578`; the cell centre is 38.875, −9.375, on land. Sintra's [parks and gardens](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/723/) motivate the choice. Location is set at deployment, with water 50; there is no birth stage.

The deployer calls `bind(hook)` once after the separate token/hook launch; the hook must return this organism and PLANT. PLANT must have fixed supply and exact-transfer balances; IMD must not rebase or charge fees. Fund by transferring IMD directly; pre-bind donations have no withdrawal path until binding.

Keepers call `settle()` to skip all ended days through the bind day in one bounded call, without weather, rewards or voting; `settle(day)` settles exactly that day and reverts if a catch-up would pass it. The first `heartbeat(maxAdvance)` requests the first full UTC day after bind, once that day ends. The callback verifies and stores; `settle()` applies the next day. The caller's IMD fee advance cannot exceed `maxAdvance` (use zero for pot-only funding). After 24 hours, anyone can `clearPending()` on an unanswered request. Timeouts and delivered incomplete results share one strike counter; retry waits six hours, and the third strike settles that day empty with a relocation READ. Maintain settlement before `max(bindDay,lastSettledDay)+30`: death is final, merges positive pot into backing and preserves exits and claims.

**One stake rule:** `stake[cell]` is PLANT parked behind that cell since the previous successful settle. A deposit made on UTC day T votes and earns for every day after T: it becomes eligible once day T itself has settled, never for a day that ended before it was parked, even when settlement lags behind the calendar. Unparked amounts drop out immediately, newest deposits first. Nothing is eligible before the first full day after bind has settled. Challenger nomination, both sides of the relocation READ, its threshold and the gardener pool all use this retained stake. A move needs strictly more than the current cell and at least `ceil((PLANT.totalSupply() − burned)/20)`. Anyone can `challenge(cell)` after maturation. READ tries the captured candidate, then the current challenger if different. Flash parking cannot gain voting power or gardening rewards. Use `park`, `unpark`, `claim()` and `redeem`; `claim(uint32[] cells)` handles historical reward cells indexed from events. Cell zero is reserved.

Hours, sips and accounting are unchanged: rain adds three water (cap 100); sun with water consumes one water and sips one tenth of available pot, with one third for eligible gardeners. Dust goes to backing. `floor()` is scaled by `1e18`; redemption pays 90% alive, 100% dead, refuses an amount that would pay nothing, and surrendered PLANT stays permanently in custody. `pot + backing + owed = IMD balance`. Spent fee advances can make pot negative; each advance unlocks at the next successful settle and remains owed until funded, without using backing or reward reserves, and funding a later request never re-locks an earlier unlocked advance. The heartbeat caller receives the unchanged 1% post-hours positive-pot bounty; failed transfers remain claimable.

Only the current oracle signer authorizes `rotate(newSigner,newIntake,newAction,nonce,deadline,sig)`, relayed by anyone. Sign `Rotate(address organism,uint256 chainId,address newSigner,address newIntake,bytes32 newAction,uint256 nonce,uint256 deadline)` in the `IdentityMD Oracle` / `2` EIP-712 domain. Expiry is `timestamp > deadline`; nonce and domain prevent replay. EOA and ERC-1271 signers are supported. Each replaced signer retains 30 days of attestation authority, never rotation authority. Current signatures and previous EOA signatures keep their raw format; for a previous contract signer, pass `abi.encodePacked(address(previousSigner), itsSignature)` in the callback's signature bytes. Pending requests keep their original Intake. Trust assumptions: oracle truth, signer contract behavior, endpoint pricing and live Intake acceptance of the unchanged JSON `guards`; the Intake delivering each answer under the request id it issued (the organism checks the pending day in the word but does not compare the attestation's own request id or question hash to its request); and the signer's rotation power, which also chooses the Intake whose price any `heartbeat` spends from the positive pot (`maxAdvance` caps only the caller's own wallet, never the pot; backing, reserves, credits and advances are unreachable).

Run `forge build` and `forge test` with the vendored dependencies. Regression, fuzz and invariant tests cover these rules, exact oracle text, protocol vectors, custody and accounting; callback/settlement stipends are 200k/400k gas. Live integration and deployment belong to the launch service.
